  <h3>Local intent: deliberately out of scope</h3>
  <p>PrepFit is a nationwide web app, not a kitchen. Local queries in this space (“meal prep near me”, “meal prep delivery Seattle”) resolve to prepared-food delivery businesses, not software, and Google answers them with a local pack PrepFit cannot enter. Chasing them would dilute the entity. The one local-adjacent question worth a line on the page is grocery availability, which the grocery-list feature already answers.</p>

  <h2>2 · Citability review</h2>
  <p>What the live AI Overview and top-ranking pages get wrong, leave out, or explain poorly.</p>
  <ol class="review">
    <li><strong>The AI Overview is a list of vendors, not an answer.</strong> On <em>best meal prep app</em> (US, 15 Sep 2026) Google’s AI Overview names MealPrepPro, Mealime, Paprika 3, Plan to Eat and Eat This Much, then ends by asking the user two follow-up questions — goal, and free vs paid. The engine is openly telling you the decision rule is missing. A page that supplies that rule in a quotable form gets pulled in.</li>
    <li><strong>Nobody separates planning from tracking.</strong> Every cited source treats “meal prep app”, “meal planner” and “macro tracker” as one category. A lifter needs a plan built <em>to</em> a macro target plus a way to verify intake against it. No top-10 page defines the difference in a sentence an engine can lift.</li>
    <li><strong>The sources are weak or stale.</strong> Of the eight AI Overview references, three are user-generated or aggregator content (Reddit, Spoon University, a YouTube lecture) and The Spruce Eats page is still headed “of 2024”. Two of the remaining five are competitor blogs ranking their own product first. There is no dietitian- or evidence-cited source in the set.</li>
    <li><strong>The numbers that matter are absent.</strong> No page in the top 10 states a protein target with a citation, states how long prepped food is safe to keep, or gives a full price table. Those are exactly the fact-shaped sentences AI engines quote.</li>
    <li><strong>PrepFit currently cannot be cited at all.</strong> The DataForSEO OnPage crawl of prepfit.fit returns a logged-out product dashboard: title “Dashboard | PrepFit”, H1 “Keep it going!”, 91 words of body text and a plain-text rate of 1.8%. There is no sentence on the site for an engine to quote.</li>
  </ol>
